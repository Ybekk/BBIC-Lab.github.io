Contact us

We welcome motivated PhD and master’s students, postdoctoral researchers, and research assistants interested in neural signal processing, machine learning, and brain-inspired intelligence.

Research with us

Our interests span brain-computer interfaces, robust neural decoding, neural representations, and brain-inspired computing. Students with backgrounds in Computer Science and Technology or Biomedical Engineering are encouraged to get in touch.

Get in touch

Please contact qiyu@zju.edu.cn to discuss your interests and potential opportunities. A short introduction to your background, research interests, and relevant work would be helpful.

You can learn more about our work through our publications and Yu Qi’s homepage.
